Your Tutor: Tshepiso

Tshepiso Msweli is the lead tutor at Elite Tutoring Co, specialising in Mathematics and Physical Sciences for Grades 10–12. With a strong foundation in these subjects from Matric and hands-on tutoring experience since 2023, Tshepiso has helped learners strengthen their understanding, improve exam performance, and gain confidence in STEM subjects.

During studies at the University of Cape Town (UCT), Tshepiso demonstrated leadership and academic initiative, taking on roles that developed organisational and mentoring skills. This experience underpins a structured, results-driven approach to tutoring, combining clear explanations, guided practice, and personalised support.

Tshepiso is committed to helping learners achieve their academic goals and build a solid foundation for further studies in science and technology.
