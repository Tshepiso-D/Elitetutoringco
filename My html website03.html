About Us

Who are we?

Elite Tutoring Co was established in 2023, and we offer a tutoring service for Grades 10, 11 and 12 in Physical Science and Mathematics.
Our teaching methods are created to allow students to understand the theory in Physical sciences and Mathematics and be able to solve questions they will find in tests and examinations.
We also believe our students can achieve any goal if they give in hard work and dedication; we make sure that all learners understand this fact.

Our Mission

We aim to make learning Physical Sciences and Mathematics an interesting experience for students in order for students to understand the theory of these subjects and it's applications and get great marks in school and be equipped with a great foundation for tertiary education.

Our Vision

We invision a world where every learner has a firm understanding of Mathematics and Physical Sciences that equips them for tertiary education regardless of the curriculum that they did in their high school education. We want a world where students and children know that any goal can be achieved if it is SMART (Specific, Measureable, Achievable, Relevant and Timeous) and if the goal is accompanied by hard work and dedication.
