About Us

Who are we?

Elite Tutoring Co was founded in 2023 to support high school learners in Grades 10, 11 and 12 with focused tutoring in Mathematics and Physical Sciences.
Our programmes are curriculum-aligned and designed to strengthen conceptual understanding, problem‑solving skills and exam readiness, so that our learners can confidently tackle class tests, assignments and final examinations.

Our Qualifications and Approach

Our lead tutor has a strong academic grounding in Mathematics and Physical Sciences and uses evidence‑based teaching techniques to help learners master core concepts.
Lessons mirror school curriculum standards, combining clear explanations with guided practice, past paper questions and regular feedback so that learners build both confidence and competence over time.

Our Educational Philosophy

We believe that every learner can succeed with the right support, structured practice and consistent feedback.
We emphasise setting SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant and Time‑bound) and pairing them with hard work and dedication, so that learners see real, measurable progress in their school results.

Impact and Results

Students who work with Elite Tutoring Co typically show noticeable improvement in their test performance, exam readiness and confidence in Mathematics and Physical Sciences.
As we continue to grow, we are actively gathering feedback, testimonials and simple before‑and‑after grade data to demonstrate the impact of our tutoring programmes for schools, parents and learners.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make Mathematics and Physical Sciences engaging and accessible, helping learners develop the skills and mindset to excel academically and prepare for tertiary education.

Our Vision

Our vision is a world where every learner we support has a strong conceptual foundation in STEM subjects and the confidence to realise their academic goals, regardless of their background or school context.
